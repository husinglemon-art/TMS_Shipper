
## Step 4: Correction Prompts

### If the planning conclusion is too weak

#### English

```text
Make the planning conclusion much stronger. The center column should clearly communicate 推荐整车直发 as the main decision, with FTL vs LTL comparison supporting that conclusion.
```

#### 中文对照

```text
请加强运输规划结论的表达。中栏需要明确把“推荐整车直发”作为主决策，并用 FTL 与 LTL 的对照来支撑这个结论。
```

### If the page becomes too list-like

#### English

```text
This page should feel analytical, not like another data list. Use cards, comparison blocks, and structured layout instead of making the whole page table-heavy.
```

#### 中文对照

```text
这个页面应该更像分析决策页，而不是另一个数据列表页。请多使用卡片、对照区块和结构化布局，不要让整页过度表格化。
```

### If the B2B tone is too weak

#### English

```text
Adjust the page to look more like an enterprise logistics planning workspace. Reduce decorative styling and make the structure more operational, clean, and decision-oriented.
```

#### 中文对照

```text
请把页面调整得更像企业级物流规划工作台。减少装饰性设计，让整体结构更运营型、更干净、更偏决策支持。
```
